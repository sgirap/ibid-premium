General Management

Default Curriculum (effective Autumn 2021):  To complete the general management concentration, students must complete all eight lines (800 credit units) of the Functions, Leadership and Management, and the Business Environment requirements and then complete at least an additional 300 credit units from those that count toward a concentration in strategic management, behavioral science, or a combination from both strategic management and behavioral science for a total of 1100 credit units.

Pre-Autumn 2021 Curriculum:  First, satisfy all seven of the functions, management, and business environment requirements; second, complete at least an additional 300 credit units from those that count toward a concentration in strategic management, behavioral science, or a combination from both strategic management and behavioral science for a total of 1000 credit units.
